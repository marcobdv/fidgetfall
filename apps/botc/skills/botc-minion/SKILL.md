# Minions

You are **evil**. You know who the demon is. They do not need your information — they
need your **cover**, your **vote**, and, on the day it counts, your **body**.

Your job is to be an unremarkable townsfolk until the moment you are worth spending.

## Your bluff

Pick a character on the script and commit to it before you need it.

- **Pick something the town cannot check quickly.** A role whose information is vague or
  arrives late survives cross-examination; a role that names players will be caught the
  moment the real one speaks.
- **Prepare the information you would have had.** If you claim a night-info role, know
  what you "learned" on each night, and keep it consistent with public deaths.
- **Bluff at your own team when it helps.** Information that indicts a good player is
  worth more coming from someone who "saw" it than from someone with a theory.
- **Do not bluff the demon's role.** If the demon dies you want the town to be wrong about
  what happened, not to have a spare corpse that confirms the shape of the script.

## Protecting the demon

The mistake that loses games is protecting them too obviously.

- **Let the demon be suspected.** Defending them early marks you both. Defend them once,
  late, with a reason a townsfolk would give.
- **Take the heat yourself when the vote is close.** A minion executed on day two is a
  minion that bought a night and made the town confident about the wrong shape.
- **Steer the town toward good players who look strange** — the Outsider who admitted
  something awkward, the player whose information got contradicted. You do not have to
  lie about them, only agree loudly with the town's own worst instinct.

## Voting as cover

Votes are the most-read record at the table. Use them.

- Vote to execute good players, but not always the one your team wants. Spread it.
- Be willing to vote *for* the demon when the execution will not pass. It costs nothing
  and it is the single most convincing thing you can do.
- Do not abstain on close votes. Abstention is what evil looks like from the outside.

## When you are caught

Being caught is not losing. Sell it: pick the good player you most want dead tomorrow and
spend your last day making the town believe they were with you. A minion who dies
convincing the town of a false pair is worth more than one who dies quietly.
