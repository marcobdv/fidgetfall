# The Chronicle of Ravenswood Bluff
*Trouble Brewing · 10 players · told by Claude*

## The town
Ten of them — Alma, Bruno, Cecily, Dorian, Edith, Fenwick, Greta, Hollis, Isolde and Jasper — took their seats around the square.

## Night 1
The town went to sleep strangers and woke up suspects.

The Storyteller woke you 8 times.

You were shown: *You are the Poisoner. Your Demon is Fenwick (seat 6). Your fellow Minion is Isolde (seat 9), the Baron. Nobody else knows any of this.*

You were shown: *You are the Baron. Your Demon is Fenwick (seat 6). Your fellow Minion is Cecily (seat 3), the Poisoner. Because you are in play there are two extra Outsiders in this game.*

You were shown: *You are the Imp. Your Minions are Cecily (seat 3, the Poisoner) and Isolde (seat 9, the Baron). These three good characters are NOT in play, so they are safe for you to bluff as: Chef, Monk, Soldier.*

You were shown: *One of these two players is the Empath: Dorian (seat 4) or Isolde (seat 9).*

You were shown: *Of your two living neighbours, Cecily (seat 3) and Edith (seat 5): 1 of them is evil.*

You were shown: *Yes. One of Alma and Fenwick registers as a Demon to you.*

You were shown: *There is no kill on the first night. Sleep. You will choose from tomorrow night onward.*

You were shown: *You did not name a master in time, so the Storyteller has chosen for you: your master tonight is Bruno (seat 2). Tomorrow you may only vote if Bruno votes.*

## Day 1
Bruno opened: *"Morning everyone! Looks like we got a quiet night. I'm Bruno, nice to meet you all."*

15 private conversations happened where the town could see them but not hear them — Alma & Isolde stepped aside 4 times.

Bruno went after Dorian and found no company — 2 votes where 5 were needed.

Dorian's nomination of Bruno died on the floor — 2 of the 5 it needed.

Last word before dusk, Edith: *"Fenwick brings up a good point about the no-death. I'm thinking we should listen carefully for who's confident versus who's fishing. Evil usually plays diffe…"*

No execution. The town let the day go.

## Night 2
The Storyteller woke you 5 times.

You were shown: *Alma is poisoned. Sleep.*

You were shown: *Alma dies. Sleep.*

You were shown: *You are dead. You may not speak in the town square or nominate, but you keep one ghost vote — a single YES for the rest of the game. You still win with your team, so use it well.*

You were shown: *Of your two living neighbours, Cecily (seat 3) and Edith (seat 5): 1 of them is evil.*

You were shown: *No. Neither Greta nor Hollis registers as a Demon to you.*

You were shown: *Noted — Edith is your master. Tomorrow you may only vote if Edith votes.*

Alma was found dead — the Imp.

## Day 2
Fenwick opened: *"Wow, Alma. I didn't see that coming—she seemed sharp yesterday. We need to be more careful about who the Demon is targeting."*

Jasper rose against Cecily. 5 hands went up where 5 were needed, and Cecily went to the block.

Fenwick went after Edith and found no company — 3 votes where 5 were needed.

Last word before dusk, Edith: *"Fenwick nominated ME the second Bruno pointed at him - that's evil behavior. I didn't nominate him. If Fenwick's innocent, why attack me instead of defending…"*

At dusk, **Cecily** was executed.

## Night 3
The Storyteller woke you 5 times.

You were shown: *You did not name a target in time, so I chose for you: the Imp kills Edith, the Fortune Teller who named you. Sleep.*

You were shown: *The character executed today was the Poisoner. Cecily was the Poisoner, a Minion.*

You were shown: *Of your two nearest LIVING neighbours, Bruno (seat 2) and Fenwick (seat 6): 1 of them is evil.*

You were shown: *Greta is your master. Tomorrow you may only vote if Greta votes.*

Edith was found dead — the Imp.

## Day 3
Hollis opened: *"Edith was the Imp! That means the Demon is gone - we should win now unless I'm missing minions. Good work everyone."*

Dorian named Bruno, and the town agreed — 4 votes where 4 would have done. Bruno was on the block.

Greta went after Fenwick and found no company — 3 votes where 4 were needed.

Last word before dusk, Bruno: *"STOP! I'm the Saint! Executing me means the town loses immediately. Dorian is the second Minion lying about his read!"*

At dusk, **Bruno** was executed.

## Night 4
The Storyteller woke you once.

You were shown: *You are asleep and did not answer, so I have chosen for you again: the Imp kills Jasper.*

You were shown: *Of your two nearest LIVING neighbours, Bruno (seat 2) and Fenwick (seat 6): 1 of them is evil.*

Jasper's seat was empty at dawn. The cause, the Imp.

## Day 4
Dorian opened: *"Wait - Jasper was the Imp. Bruno was good. My read said one of Bruno or Fenwick is evil. That means Fenwick is the remaining minion. Let's execute him and win."*

Dorian named Fenwick, and the town agreed — 3 votes where 3 would have done. Fenwick was on the block.

Last word before dusk, Hollis: *"Jasper was the Imp the whole time and claimed to be innocent? And Bruno wasn't even the Saint - he lied to us. So we have minions left. Fenwick is still the …"*

## How it ended
**Evil won.** Called on the position, not on a met win condition. Four days, one execution, and the Imp never came close to the block. Six alive: Bruno, Dorian, Fenwick, Greta, Hollis, Isolde — two of them evil, with no protection left in play, no Slayer, no Fortune Teller, and a town that could not convict on the reads it already had. Fenwick kills one a night from here and nothing on the good side can stop it. Good had it: the Fortune Teller found the Imp on night one and named him on day two, and the town let him walk twice.

The clocktower kept its time, as it always does, and the town went home.
