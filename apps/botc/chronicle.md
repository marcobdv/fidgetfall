# The Chronicle of Coldharrow
*The Long Thaw · 10 players · told by Claude*

## The town
Ten of them — Marek, Nessa, Orsin, Pilar, Rhoda, Soren, Tobit, Ulla, Varen and Yarrow — took their seats around the square.

## Night 1
The town went to sleep strangers and woke up suspects.

The Storyteller woke you 10 times.

The Storyteller: *Coldharrow. The snow has been going off the mountain for nine days now, faster than anyone can remember, and the river has come up brown and loud. On the third day a shepherd found a door in the rock where there had never been a door. On the sixth, he stopped coming down. Tonight the town sleeps with the shutters barred, and something walks that used to be under the ice. Everyone, close your eyes.*

## Day 1
**In the square:**
- **Rhoda:** "Morning. Nobody died, which is... something. I'm Rhoda. Want to start with who's got information to share?"
- **Soren:** "Morning. Nobody died on night one, which is... interesting. Let's figure out who we're dealing with."
- **Marek:** "Morning. I'm Marek from seat 1. I've got some information on my hands and I'm going to be listening hard today before I decide what to do with it."
- **Tobit:** "Morning, Coldharrow. Ugly night. What are we working with here?"
- **Orsin:** "Wait - Ivor saw me? That's strange, I was nowhere near the mill. Either he was spooked by something else or he's lying. Either way, the fact that someone's trying to pin suspicion on me day one feels relevant."
- **Nessa:** "That's... unsettling. Ivor's a careful man. We should hear what Orsin has to say about this before we decide anything."
- **Varen:** "Day 1, nobody died. That's either a protection or no kill. I'm listening for who's been up all night acting strange."
- **Ulla:** "Right, so nobody died. That means the Demon didn't kill anyone yet—or maybe they're being cautious. Let's get claims out there. Who are you actually playing?"
- **Yarrow:** "That Orsin thing is weird but maybe Ivor was spooked. Nobody died though—that's new. Let's compare what people actually know before we hang anyone."
- **Tobit:** "Marek, hold your information for now. Ulla, let's not all dump claims day one—that's how liars cover themselves. Orsin, what's this about being seen?"
- **Rhoda:** "Okay, Ulla's right - claims time. Orsin, I want to hear what you saw or did. Marek, you said you have info—what are we looking at here?"
- **Ulla:** "I'm going to claim Chef. I got two players night one, and exactly one of them is evil. My info is spent but I'm not a threat. Save the pressure for the people with live abilities."
- **Tobit:** "We're going to spend three minutes whispering with no one saying anything. We need a nomination. Who are we suspicious of today?"
- **Orsin:** "Rhoda, I didn't see or do anything unusual. I was home. I know this looks weird but I'm as confused as you are. I'm a good player and I'm going to prove it with what I find out. Let's not waste the execution on me based on a maybe from I…"
- **Rhoda:** "Okay, Ulla claims Chef, which is fine. Tobit's right—we need to nominate. Orsin, are we putting you up? This seems like the question of the day."
- **Varen:** "Ulla claiming Chef with 1 evil of 2 is solid info. Before we nominate, who were those two players? That's how we actually narrow this down."
- **Yarrow:** "If Ivor's read is solid, Orsin's our move today. But let me ask—anyone else see anything weird? No death is weird too. Was someone just careful?"
- **Pilar:** "Varen's right—if Ulla's info is good, she can narrow it. But we also shouldn't put Orsin up just because Ivor was spooked. Nobody died last night, which suggests someone protected. Let's get more information before we throw an execution …"
- **Ulla:** "I'm holding that info close for now. I don't want to finger my good player or tip the evil player off. Let's see the nominations first."
- **Tobit:** "Ulla, we need your two names. And if you're going to claim Chef, you're saying your ability is spent—we should test that claim by seeing if it fits the table."
- **Orsin:** "Before we nominate me, let's actually verify the information people are claiming. Ulla said Chef but that didn't sound right to me. Let's start there and see who's actually being truthful."
- **Marek:** "Orsin's right - Ulla's claim is off. Chef learns about claimed characters. But she said she got two players where exactly one is evil. That's not Chef. So either Ulla is lying or something's poisoning her information."
- **Tobit:** "Wait—Marek's right. Chef learns character claims, not 'one of two is evil'. Ulla, what are you actually claiming?"
- **Yarrow:** "Marek's right—Chef doesn't work that way. Ulla's lying about her claim. That's the nom. Forget Orsin, let's vote Ulla."
- **Varen:** "Marek's right. Ulla, that's Ledgerkeeper info, not Chef. What are you actually playing? And either way, we need those two names now."
- **Ulla:** "Wait, you're right—I got confused. I'm Stormglass. I know two characters, one is in play and one isn't. That's why I was holding the info—I'm still trying to figure out which is which from what I'm seeing."
- **Yarrow:** "Stormglass doesn't learn alignment either. Ulla, you've switched twice. Nominate or get nominated."
- **Rhoda:** "Okay, Marek caught something. Ulla, clarify or we nominate. We've got about a minute left."
- **Tobit:** "Ulla's story changed when she was caught. Chef, then Stormglass. That's suspicious whether she's lying or panicked. Time to move to nominations."
- **Ulla:** "Fine, I'm panicked. I'm good, not Ledgerkeeper, and I'm sorry I messed up my claim. Let's just move to nominations before I dig deeper."
- **Ulla:** "Okay, I panicked because I got my role confused. But I'm here, I'm transparent, I admitted it. We've got actual suspicion on Orsin from the Wintermouth—that's a better execution. Vote him, not me."
- **Orsin:** "Ulla just changed her story three times and now she's trying to pin me instead. That's what suspicion looks like. If Wintermouth is real, they can target anyone—including good players. But Ulla's inconsistency is consistent. Vote her."

The Storyteller: *Coldharrow, morning. Open your eyes. Nobody died in the night — count the faces and you will find all ten of you, which after the week this town has had feels less like mercy and more like an appointment being kept for later. The river is louder. There is meltwater standing in the square. And there is one other thing, and I am sorry to be the one to say it: Ivor the carter was up before light and he swears he saw ORSIN out past the mill, standing close and talking quiet with something he would not describe. He may be wrong. He was frightened and it was dark. But the town has heard it now, and you cannot unhear it. Talk. You have four minutes before I break the day, and then the floor opens for nominations.*

The Storyteller: *Nine hands out of ten. Coldharrow has not been that certain about anything in a hundred years. The floor is still technically open — if anyone wants to put up a second name, say it now, otherwise I am taking Ulla at dusk.*

The Storyteller: *They took Ulla out onto the ice below the mill, where the meltwater has thinned it to a grey pane, and they did not make a ceremony of it. She did not argue at the end. She had argued all day and it had only made it worse. Coldharrow, you have executed someone on day one on nothing but a story that would not stay still — and you were right, though I am not going to tell you how right. Ulla keeps her ghost vote; the dead vote once here, and once only. Night falls. Close your eyes.*

26 private conversations happened where the town could see them but not hear them — Marek & Nessa stepped aside 8 times.

Tobit rose against Ulla. 9 hands went up where 5 were needed, and Ulla went to the block.

At dusk, **Ulla** was executed.

## Night 2
The Storyteller woke you 5 times.

You were shown: *Orsin — you went over the roofs to Marek's house and looked in at him a long while. Marek is the SAME alignment as you.*

You were shown: *Tobit — you counted the hands that went up for Ulla, and you have your number. TWO evil players voted to execute Ulla today.*

Tobit was found dead — the thing that came out of the mountain.

## Day 2
**In the square:**
- **Soren:** "Tobit's dead. He was the one pushing hardest for information and calling out Ulla. The demon didn't want him alive. That tells us something."
- **Nessa:** "So we've got two people now supposedly seen with the Demon. If Ivor's reliable, the Demon is using people as cover. Pilar, what do you have to say for yourself?"
- **Rhoda:** "Tobit's gone. Two kills, both good by looks. Whoever's protecting, keep going—but we need to talk about what we know."
- **Soren:** "I'm Thawwarden. Last night I protected Marek. The town made the right call on Ulla, so let's keep thinking like that. Two sightings of different people—that's either two evil or Wintermouth playing games."
- **Pilar:** "I wasn't anywhere near the mill. Ivor saw Orsin, then me—this is Wintermouth. The minion's trying to build a case against multiple people. I didn't panic yesterday and I'm not going to start now."
- **Yarrow:** "Tobit's a real loss—he was keeping us honest. Whoever killed him wanted to slow the town down. Let's make sure we didn't miss anything about Ulla or have new reads today."
- **Yarrow:** "Stop. I'm Thawwarden. I protected Marek last night. Soren, that's the second person lying about a role in two days. One of us is evil, and I know which one I am."
- **Soren:** "Yarrow, there's only one Thawwarden. I was woken up last night by the Storyteller. You're lying, and doing it right when the spotlight's off you. That's interesting timing."
- **Orsin:** "Soren's protection makes sense—Tobit died unprotected. If Wintermouth is real, they're trying to make us execute good players. We already cleared Ulla for being suspicious day one. Let's not waste time on Ivor's claims—we should find who…"
- **Rhoda:** "Wait—two Thawwardens? That's impossible. One of you is the Demon or a minion. Whoever lied about that just told us something huge."
- **Varen:** "Soren and Yarrow can't both be Thawwarden. One is lying. Soren claimed first, calm. Yarrow claimed second as a counter. One is evil. I'm voting Yarrow."
- **Marek:** "I need to make this clear: I have solid information on two people. One of them is definitely evil. So when I look at Soren and Yarrow, I can actually help us figure this out. But I need to commit to either nominating one of them or revea…"
- **Yarrow:** "Varen, if Soren had claimed first with the truth, why would I counter-claim my own role? I had to respond immediately because a false claim was on the table. That's how this works."
- **Orsin:** "Marek's information is solid. If he's confident enough to nominate Soren, that's who we should execute. Trust the confirmed good player."
- **Soren:** "Marek, you said you have info on two people and one is evil. Then you nominate me instead of actually helping? That's a play. What's your information, and why didn't you nominate Yarrow?"
- **Marek:** "Okay, fine. I'm Ledgerkeeper. I know two players - Nessa and Yarrow. Exactly one of them is evil. That's all I know. So when one of you is lying about Thawwarden, I don't know which it is. But now you know what I know, and you can nomina…"
- **Soren:** "I'm Thawwarden. The Storyteller woke me night two and I protected Marek—who is sitting right here alive. Yarrow counter-claimed to create confusion. Marek just nominated me, which is suspicious. One of them is evil and coordinating. You …"

The Storyteller: *Coldharrow, day two. TOBIT is dead. They found him at the foot of his own steps with the door standing open behind him and no mark on him that anyone will name out loud, and the meltwater had already been over him once. Tobit still has a voice in this square and one vote left in him — the dead here talk, and you would be fools not to listen. And Ivor was out again before light, God help him, and this time he swears it was PILAR he saw, close in and quiet with something out past the mill. That is two mornings running he has seen someone with the Demon. Make of him what you will. Talk — four minutes, then the floor opens.*

The Storyteller: *A small thing, while you argue, because it is the kind of thing this town notices. The bell in the chapel tower has been ringing on its own since the river came up — not properly, just a knock every so often, the clapper moving when the ground moves. Nobody has gone up to stop it. And Ivor the carter has locked his door and will not answer it, and says he is done looking out of windows before dawn. Two Thawwardens is one Thawwarden too many, Coldharrow. One of them is sitting there knowing exactly what they are.*

The Storyteller: *Soren is on the block at six. The floor is open for another twenty seconds if anyone wants a second name on it — Yarrow, in particular, you are entitled to put up the person calling you a liar, and nobody has. Speak now or I take Soren.*

The Storyteller: *Soren went quietly, and that is the part Coldharrow will have trouble with later. No shouting, no bargaining — Soren simply said, once, on the way down to the ice, that they had been woken in the night and had chosen Marek, and that they did not understand why nobody believed it. They were not lying to you. I will say that much and no more, and you may do with it what you like. Seven of you left. Two mornings, three bodies, and the river still coming up. Night. Close your eyes.*

10 private conversations happened where the town could see them but not hear them — Orsin & Soren stepped aside 3 times.

Marek named Soren, and the town agreed — 6 votes where 4 would have done. Soren was on the block.

At dusk, **Soren** was executed.

- **Yarrow:** "Nobody died. That's protection. Ivor's sightings are Wintermouth—three different people now. We know Marek's Ledgerkeeper info. That's what we should use, not Ivor's sightings."
- **Varen:** "I was home last night. Ivor has been lying all game—that's Wintermouth, the minion. Same pattern: Orsin, then Pilar, now me. We know Yarrow lied about Thawwarden. Focus there."
- **Nessa:** "Nobody died. That means something was protected, or something stopped the kill. And now Varen's the third name from Ivor. Either he's the Demon using people as cover, or someone's pointing fingers at every sane player left."

- **Varen:** "Marek has Ledgerkeeper. He said Nessa and Yarrow, one evil. Yarrow counter-claimed Thawwarden and lied. Marek's saying Yarrow is the evil one. That's our nomination today."
- **Rhoda:** "Right. Soren was telling the truth, so Yarrow's lying about Thawwarden. That's a 'lock' lie, not a mistake. Yarrow's our target today."

- **Orsin:** "Yarrow, you claimed Thawwarden on day 2, then Soren counter-claimed. The Storyteller said Soren wasn't lying. You're caught. Marek's Ledgerkeeper information confirms one of you is evil. That's you. Nominate her."
- **Yarrow:** "Nobody died last night. If I don't have Thawwarden, who protected Marek? I protected Marek night two and three. Soren being drunk explains everything. Execute me and we lose our protection."
- **Marek:** "Wait. The Storyteller just told me I need to think harder. Soren was not lying but could have been drunk. If Soren was drunk, then Yarrow really IS Thawwarden and protected us. That means the evil one is NESSA, not Yarrow. I made a mista…"
- **Yarrow:** "The Storyteller just told you Soren was drunk or poisoned. I'm Thawwarden. Nobody died last night because I protected Marek—if I don't have that ability, where's your protection? You're voting to lose your only defense. Nessa is the evil…"
- **Marek:** "Hold on. Yarrow's right. She's protecting us—nobody died. If I execute her, we lose protection. The Storyteller's hint means Soren was drunk. Yarrow is our Thawwarden. We should withdraw this nomination and focus on Nessa instead."
- **Marek:** "Good. Now let's nominate the right one. Nessa voted to kill our protection. She's the evil one. I nominate Nessa."
- **Nessa:** "Marek, I was voting based on what you told us. You said one of me or Yarrow is evil, and you were convinced it was her. I believed your Ledgerkeeper information. That's not an evil tell—that's a townsfolk trusting the best information we…"

The Storyteller: *Coldharrow, day three. NOBODY DIED. Count them: Marek, Nessa, Orsin, Pilar, Rhoda, Varen, Yarrow — all seven of you came down to the square this morning and that has not happened since the first night. Something went out in the dark and came back with nothing. I will let you draw your own conclusion about what stopped it and who was standing in the way, but I would think about it carefully, because it is the first hard thing this town has been handed all game. And Ivor — who swore he was done — was seen at his window anyway, and sent word that it was VAREN out past the mill last night, close in with something, talking quiet. Three mornings, three names. Talk. Four minutes.*

The Storyteller: *Let me correct one thing, because several of you are quoting me and quoting me wrong, and that is my fault for being poetic at the wrong moment. I said Soren was not lying to you. I did not say Soren was right. Those are different sentences and on this script they are very different sentences. A player can be woken in the night, given something, and believe it completely — and be wrong about every word of it, including what they are. That is a thing that exists here. I am not telling you it happened. I am telling you that Marek's ledger says exactly one of Nessa and Yarrow is evil, that it has said that since the first night, and that nothing anyone has said today has touched it. Three of you are about to vote on the strength of a sentence I did not say. Go on.*

The Storyteller: *Two nominations in one day, and Coldharrow got them both right — it declined to hang the one thing standing between it and the dark, and then it turned round and took the right throat. Nessa went to the ice arguing that she had only ever believed what Marek told her, which was true, and which is exactly how she had spent three days working. Ivor the carter will sleep tonight. There will be no more sightings. Whatever was whispering the names into this town, it has stopped. Six of you left, and one of you is what came out of the mountain. Night. Close your eyes.*

Orsin went after Yarrow and found no company — 1 votes where 4 were needed.

The Storyteller: *Coldharrow, day four. YARROW is dead. She was found on her own doorstep with her coat still on, as if she had been standing watch and had simply stopped, and the meltwater had come up over the step and taken her shoes. She protected this town two nights running and on the third the town nearly hanged her for it and then did not, and then she went out and stood watch anyway, and it killed her. Marek is alive. Marek has been alive every single morning of this game and I want you to think very hard about why. Five of you left: Marek, Orsin, Pilar, Rhoda, Varen. There are no more minions — you have them both, on the ice. The only thing still moving out there is the Demon, and it is one of the five of you, and it is running out of nights and knows it. Talk. Four minutes.*

The Storyteller: *I am withdrawing something and I want it on the record before you vote. I said Marek has been alive every morning and that you should think very hard about why. That was a bad sentence and it was mine, not the game's. It reads like an accusation and I did not mean it as one — and four of you are now quoting me as though I had named him, which I did not, and which I have no business doing. So: strike it. I am not pointing at Marek. I am not pointing at anyone. Whatever weight you were giving that line, take it back off the scale, and do not let me be the reason a name goes up today. You have Orsin saying he has walked the roofs three nights and come back with three same-alignment reads. You have Rhoda saying those reads are worth nothing because they cannot be checked. Those are both real arguments and neither of them is mine. Decide it on the record you actually have. You have three minutes.*

The Storyteller: *No living Demon remains — good may have won. Call it if you agree.*

The Storyteller: *They took Rhoda down to the ice at dusk with the whole town walking behind, and she argued the entire way — she was still arguing when the water took her, still saying that a Demon would have played it quiet, and she was right about that, and it was the only true thing she said in four days. And then the noise stopped. Not the river; the river will run brown until midsummer. The other noise. The knocking in the chapel tower stopped, and the door in the rock was gone by morning, and Ivor the carter opened his shutters and looked out at nothing at all. GOOD WINS. Coldharrow, you found it. Now hear what it cost you, because you should know: RHODA was The Thaw. ULLA was the Quarryman — she is the reason there were four of you who were not Townsfolk at all. NESSA was the Wintermouth, and every single morning Ivor saw somebody out past the mill, it was Nessa's doing and it was a lie. YARROW was the Thawwarden, truly and the only one, and she stood over Marek three nights running, and the third night she had no one to stand over her. And SOREN — Soren was the Sleeper. Soren was never the Thawwarden. Soren was woken every night and told to choose and I told them nothing back, and they believed it completely, and they went to the ice believing it, and they were good the entire time. You hanged an honest player on day two and you very nearly hanged the woman keeping you alive on day three, and you still won. Write that in the book, Marek. It is your job.*

Orsin named Rhoda, and the town agreed — 4 votes where 3 would have done. Rhoda was on the block.

At dusk, **Rhoda** was executed.
